Megenious Book Shop – Magical FAQ

Where imagination gallops free and unicorns guard every shelf.

1. Where is Megenious Book Shop located?

We are proudly located in the heart of Dreamland, just past the Rainbow Bridge and two steps left of the Floating Castle. If you see a unicorn grazing nearby, you're close!

2. What kind of books do you offer?

We specialize in imaginary, mythical, enchanted, and other-worldly books — stories that exist only in Dreamland and cannot be found in the human world.
Expect titles about unicorn quests, dragon diplomacy, mermaid poetry, time-bending adventures, and more magical wonders.

3. Do you have a favorite theme?

Yes — Unicorns!
Our unicorn collection includes rare spellbound manuscripts, glitter-sealed journals, and epic adventures featuring the gentlest magical creatures in Dreamland.

4. Can I purchase books from the real world?

Sadly, no. Real-world books cannot cross the Dreamland border without losing their letters. We exclusively sell books born from imagination.

5. Do you offer home delivery?

Yes!
Orders within Dreamland are delivered via:

Flying scroll owls,

Rainbow-trail unicorn couriers, or

Teleporting book portals (for premium members).

Physical-world delivery is currently not available due to dimensional customs regulations.

6. Are your books safe for all ages?

Absolutely. All books pass through our Unicorn Harmony Filter, ensuring every story is safe, uplifting, and filled with positive magic.
